Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About SimpliTrain
and LMS for Aviation

An aviation TMS is the operational backbone of an in-house training department. It manages recurrent training schedules, tracks every crew certification and expiry date, coordinates instructors and simulator slots, and produces audit-ready records for EASA, FAA, ICAO, and IATA inspectors. SimpliTrain combines LMS, TMS, and LXP in a single platform — so you manage eLearning delivery, instructor-led scheduling, and compliance tracking without switching systems.
SimpliTrain tracks every recurrence cycle — type rating renewals, operator proficiency checks, line checks, annual emergency procedures, CRM refreshers, and dangerous goods training. Automated alerts fire at 90, 60, and 30 days before any expiry. The built-in scheduler plans recurrent sessions across bases and fleets without conflicts, and records sync automatically when crew complete training — online or offline.
Yes. SimpliTrain is designed for multi-base, multi-fleet operations. Training managers at headquarters see full crew currency status across every location from one dashboard. Fleet-specific training tracks — A320 versus B737 type ratings — are managed separately and assigned automatically based on crew qualification and base. No separate logins or disconnected systems required.
SimpliTrain generates complete compliance audit reports in under 60 seconds — filterable by crew member, role, aircraft type, base, or date range. Reports are formatted for direct submission to your Competent Authority. Your team no longer spends days pulling records manually before an inspection. The audit trail is live, accurate, and exportable at any time.
Yes. SimpliTrain supports delivery and tracking of EASA Part-147 Basic Training, Type Training, and OJT completion. It covers the 2024–2025 regulatory updates — including formally recognised Distance Learning (async CBT) and Virtually Controlled Environments (synchronous vILT), updated SMS content requirements for Modules 9 and 10, and the new MCQ assessment formats.
SimpliTrain's built-in scheduler handles simulator slot allocation, instructor assignment, and classroom capacity planning alongside crew training schedules. You see utilisation gaps and conflicts before they reach operations — reducing wasted simulator time and last-minute rebooking costs.
Yes. Crew can complete assigned eLearning modules without an internet connection — on long-haul flights, in remote hangars, or at off-grid maintenance sites. Completion records, assessment scores, and progress data sync automatically when the device reconnects. No training record is ever lost.
SimpliTrain supports SCORM 1.2, xAPI (Tin Can API), and cmi5 — the full range of aviation eLearning authoring outputs. Upload content from Articulate, Adobe Captivate, or any compliant authoring tool. SimpliTrain also includes a built-in AI course builder to convert SOPs, safety bulletins, and airworthiness directives into SCORM-ready modules without external software.
SimpliTrain uses flat-rate per-admin pricing. You pay based on how many administrators manage the platform — not how many crew members use it. A fleet that grows from 500 to 5,000 crew members pays the same platform fee. Plans start from $60 per admin per month. Free trial available, no credit card required.
Most aviation operators are fully live in under two weeks. Existing training records, crew data, and course content can be migrated during onboarding with no IT team required. SimpliTrain's onboarding specialists handle configuration and setup, and your first crew training can be assigned within 48 hours of go-live.
